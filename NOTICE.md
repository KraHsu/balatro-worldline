Balatro is a game by LocalThunk, published by Playstack. This is an unofficial
mod and is not endorsed by the game developer or publisher. You need your own
copy of Balatro to use it.

The mod loads card art, fonts and shaders from the running game. Those assets
and the Balatro executable are not distributed in the mod packages. Images in
`docs/images` are English and Chinese screenshots of a synthetic test worldline; the game's artwork
shown in them is not covered by this project's MIT license.

[Steamodded](https://github.com/Steamodded/smods) and
[Lovely](https://github.com/ethangreen-dev/lovely-injector) are separate projects
with their own licenses. They are not bundled here. The test workflow checks out
Steamodded solely to load its JSON library. Python development dependencies are
listed in `requirements-dev.txt` and remain under their respective licenses.
